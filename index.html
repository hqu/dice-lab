Classroom Dice Lab

100 students each roll two dice five times. Switch on a scenario to see how one small rule shifts the whole class.

Rich get richer If a student's first roll totals more than 10 (an 11 or 12), every later die that shows a 1 or 2 is rolled again once, and the new number is kept. Everyone else rolls as normal.
Gender bias Students 1 to 50 are male and students 51 to 100 are female. Every die a female student rolls has 0.5 subtracted from its value, so her total drops by 5 points over ten dice. Male students are unchanged.
